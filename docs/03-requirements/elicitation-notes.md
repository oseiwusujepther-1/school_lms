# Requirements Elicitation Notes

## Status

No stakeholder interviews or elicitation sessions have been recorded yet. This file tracks questions to investigate; it does not claim that stakeholder feedback has already been collected.

## Intended participants

Seek input from representative students and teachers. Include school administrators or technical/support staff only if discovery confirms they influence user access, school policies, hosting, or support.

## Open questions

| Topic | Question | Why it matters | Status |
|-------|----------|----------------|--------|
| Current problem | Where do students and teachers currently find, share, and submit course work? What is most difficult about that process? | Validates the problem and helps prioritize features. | Open |
| Course access | How are courses created, and how are students and teachers associated with them? | Defines course membership and access-control requirements. | Open |
| Accounts and roles | Who creates accounts and assigns roles? Are there existing school accounts to use? | Affects onboarding, authorization, and possible integrations. | Open |
| Materials | What types of learning materials are needed, and should they be uploaded, linked, or both? | Determines material metadata, storage, and access needs. | Open |
| Assignments | Which assignment details are essential (for example, instructions, due dates, or attachments)? | Defines teacher workflow and what students need to see. | Open |
| Submissions | What submission formats are needed? Are resubmissions or late submissions allowed? | Defines student workflow, storage needs, and edge cases. | Open |
| Review | Do teachers need to provide feedback or grades in the first increment? | Sets the boundary between submission review and assessment features. | Open |
| Privacy | Which users may view course materials and individual submissions? How long should data be kept? | Informs access control, data handling, and retention requirements. | Open |
| Accessibility | What accessibility needs and standards should the product support? | Makes accessibility expectations specific and verifiable. | Open |
| Operations | Which devices, network conditions, user volumes, and availability expectations should be supported? | Informs platform, performance, and deployment requirements. | Open |

## Turning findings into requirements

For each useful finding, record the affected user and need, the proposed requirement, its priority, acceptance criteria, and any assumptions or dependencies. Update the SRS and feature requirements when a change is agreed, and preserve unresolved items here until the team has enough evidence to decide.
